# Frame-level possession estimation

## Approach and assets

The algorithm combines local tracking evidence with match context and decodes each half as a sequence of home possession, away possession, or dead ball. The sequence model discourages short, unsupported changes, which would split otherwise coherent possessions in the ranking metric.

- **Tactical automatic tracking.** The metadata maps track slots to teams. Each row supplies ball and player positions when available. Coordinates are scaled to meters and the one-based tracking ID is shifted to the video's zero-based frame index. We compute the distance from the ball to each team's closest player and the fraction of tracked players moving at least 2 m/s when enough speeds are available. We reduce control evidence for fast balls and for duels where both teams are almost equally close.
- **Tactical Smart Tagging.** Automatically generated attack phases supply weak evidence for the tagged team, and selected defensive phases supply weak evidence for the opposition. Conflicting tags reduce the net team signal. Set pieces provide candidate restart windows. The algorithm looks for ball motion beginning near a plausible location and uses the tagged side when known. The published half ranges define where the decoder runs.
- **Asset choices.** The implementation uses the tactical timeline throughout. It requires neither video decoding nor alignment to the broadcast recording. Homography is unnecessary for the distances we compute from pitch coordinates.

The predictor uses fixed heuristics expressed in meters, speeds, and time. It uses no training data, external match data, or manual annotations. Synthetic scenarios test behavior. The organizer's public sample was used once the predictor was fixed, as a pipeline check.

## Possession rules

Evidence is aggregated in five-frame blocks, about 0.2 seconds, and decoded using Viterbi dynamic programming. The best sequence balances tracking and tagging evidence against the cost of changing state.

- **Live play and handovers.** Close, sustained contact with a clear advantage over an opponent supports control. A direct handover requires contact on the pitch or stronger tagging evidence for the receiving team at that point. Equal tag evidence can support live play while the previous team retains possession. A brief touch contributes little compared with the cost of a team switch.
- **Stoppages and restarts.** Tracking outside the pitch, sustained stationary play, low player activity, and proximity to an automatic restart support dead ball. Ball absence alone provides little evidence. For a tagged start of half, the estimated kickoff supports a dead-ball lead-in. A restart makes it easier for the decoder to return to live play, using the likely restarting side and the available tracking evidence.
- **Output.** Each decoded block writes its predicted label to every frame it covers. Frames outside the published half ranges receive `X`. A stoppage receives `D` regardless of the restarting side.

## Limitations

The model can miss a whistle or foul that leaves the ball moving, a restart absent from the automatic tags, or a controlled one-touch pass. A long occlusion can hide an actual handover. Noisy ball positions near the touchline can produce false stoppages. Five-frame blocks limit the precision of transition boundaries. A future version could use a ball-flight model or video cues for contacts and whistles.

## Verification

The organizer's validator accepted the full-match CSV. It has 199,319 rows from frame 0 through 199,318. Two independent runs on the tactical data generated byte-identical CSVs. On the public five-minute pipeline check, the official scorer reported 0.4414 spellF1 and 0.6941 macroF1. It counted 12 predicted possessions across 17 reference possessions in that window. The organizer scores the full match against private labels.

## Reproduction

Use Python 3.13.9 with the three tactical files listed in [README.md](README.md). There are no third-party dependencies or model weights. Run:

```sh
python src/main.py --data-dir /path/to/match-bundle --output output/TEAM_NAME.csv
```

Check the generated CSV with the organizer's `evaluation/validate_submission.py`.
